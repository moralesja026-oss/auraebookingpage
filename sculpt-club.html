WHAT IS SCULPT CLUB

Community First.

Sculpt Club is a free, weekly outdoor sculpt session open to everyone. No membership, no sign-up fee, no strings attached — just 45 minutes of intentional, beat-driven movement in the open air with people who want to feel good.

Every week brings a different music genre and training focus, so no two sessions ever feel the same. Whether you're a seasoned athlete or stepping into sculpt for the first time, there's a place for you here.

HOW IT WORKS

Every Week, Something New.

♫

Fresh Genres

From Latin rhythms to hip-hop beats to house music — the soundtrack changes weekly to keep your energy high and your body guessing.

⚖

Rotating Focus

Each session targets a different area — full body, lower body burn, core intensive, upper body sculpt — so you build balanced strength over time.

☆

All Levels

Modifications for beginners, progressions for advanced. Everyone moves at their own pace in a supportive, judgement-free environment.
